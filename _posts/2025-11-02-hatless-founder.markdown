A common mistake made by those in senior positions at big companies is that they forget to remove their hat — they bring their enterprise-scale mindset everywhere.

A Principal Data Scientist at a VC-funded fintech company collaborated with a colleague on a project. Each took different approaches to analyze a client's data and generate reports for them.

One approach involved loading the data into a spreadsheet and discovering simple actionable insights in just a few hours. It was approximate, unscalable, and rudimentary. Yet, the client found it useful.

The Principal Data Scientist spent an entire week building complex data models and calculations on Jupyter Notebook, but didn't share any clear insights or actionables. This approach was accurate, scalable, and advanced. But the output was upside down.

> **Sometimes, the right tool is the one that gets you answers today, not the one that scales tomorrow.** Leave the hat at the door.
